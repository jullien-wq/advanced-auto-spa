1. Executive Summary
The redesign should be presented as an elevation, not a rejection of the existing brand. The current site already contains valuable ingredients: real work, an owner-operated story, public service information, a current Manalapan address, strong visual content, and a substantial Google reputation. The opportunity is to organize those assets into a clearer local-search and booking system.
What is working
The site clearly identifies auto detailing in Manalapan and promotes hand wash, detailing, ceramic coating, paint correction, and related services.
The business story is differentiated: owner-operated, purpose-built studio, professional lighting, and personal involvement in each vehicle.
Real photography and public pricing provide stronger trust than many generic local-detailing websites.
The review reputation is a major competitive advantage and should be the strongest conversion asset on the site.
What is holding the site back
Old Matawan information is still visible on third-party sources while the current site uses Manalapan.
Review counts, years-in-business language, and some public facts are not presented consistently across the digital ecosystem.
Duplicate and legacy service URLs divide clarity and can dilute search signals.
Wix service templates repeat price and duration information and rely on generic links.
The homepage and detailing hub overlap in keyword intent instead of having distinct jobs.
The gallery and video sections contain substantial proof but are not organized as fast, searchable case studies.
The contact and booking journey does not qualify leads or guide different customer types effectively.
Accessibility issues weaken image understanding, link clarity, form usability, and keyboard/screen-reader confidence.
Strategic conclusion
Do not lead with dozens of new blog posts. First correct entity consistency, consolidate URLs, standardize business data, rebuild the main page templates, improve accessibility, and connect the website to measurable Google Business Profile actions.

Google local visibility is primarily shaped by relevance, distance, and prominence. Advanced Auto Spa cannot control the searcher’s distance, but it can strengthen relevance and prominence by presenting one consistent business entity across the website, Google Business Profile, maps, social profiles, and major data sources.
Create one master business record and use it everywhere.
Correct or suppress the former Matawan address on BBB, directory, map, and data-aggregator listings.
Publish a temporary “Now Located in Manalapan” page if relocation confusion is still affecting customers.
Use a dedicated Contact & Location page with written directions, parking/drop-off instructions, map, storefront photo, and current hours.
Keep the Google Business Profile name limited to the real-world business name; do not append keywords unless they are genuinely part of signage and branding.

Choose one preferred windshield rock-chip repair URL and permanently redirect the duplicate.

5. Accessibility Analysis
Scope note
This is a preliminary public-content review, not a legal certification. Full WCAG conformance requires manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, reflow, contrast, form, booking, gallery, and mobile testing.
Confirmed or strongly indicated issues
Many gallery images expose only generic “Image” labels, while other pages use irrelevant stock-style descriptions such as “Foggy Pier.”
Some service images repeat unrelated “Gold Package Hand Wash Manalapan NJ” alternative text on Maintenance Detail and Windshield Repair pages.
Generic “Read More,” “Book It,” and “Book Now” links are ambiguous when read outside their visual context.
Duplicate price and duration fields can be announced twice by assistive technology.
The video page lacks meaningful crawlable summaries, captions, and transcripts.
WCAG 2.2 AA requirements for the rebuild
Semantic landmarks, logical headings, and a skip-to-content link.
Keyboard-accessible navigation, filters, before/after controls, gallery, lightbox, forms, and booking.
Visible focus states and focus order that preserves meaning.
Accurate alt text for meaningful images and empty alt for decorative images.
Text and control contrast that meets WCAG requirements.
Labels that remain visible, clear instructions, error summaries, and accessible confirmation states.
Captions and transcripts for video.
Responsive reflow and 200% zoom without loss of content or functionality.
Reduced-motion support and no scroll-jacking.
No accessibility overlay used as a substitute for accessible design and code.
